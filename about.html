About the company

BlindsXpert Malaysia

Window blinds and solutions, with a focus on product development and customer requirements.

Established 2005

Customized and ready-made blinds

BlindsXpert Malaysia was established in 2005. The company supplies customized and ready-made products and describes an eco-friendly focus and continued development of blinds technologies.

BlindsXpert also provides installation and site measurement in KL and Selangor, as well as free consultation and quotation.

The original company website lists Malaysian and international schools, IPTA/IPTS, government, private companies and individuals among its customer groups.
